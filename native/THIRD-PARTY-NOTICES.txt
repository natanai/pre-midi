Native 4x drive resampling reference

The streaming polyphase implementation in drive.hpp follows the Blackman-windowed sinc filter definitions in Chromium/Blink up_sampler.cc and down_sampler.cc. The implementation uses its own fixed streaming histories and does not link Chromium.

Sources:
https://github.com/chromium/chromium/blob/main/third_party/blink/renderer/platform/audio/up_sampler.cc
https://github.com/chromium/chromium/blob/main/third_party/blink/renderer/platform/audio/down_sampler.cc

Native audio decoding

Symphonia 0.6.1: MPL-2.0. The unchanged decoder and demuxer source is available at https://crates.io/crates/symphonia/0.6.1 and https://github.com/pdeljanov/Symphonia. The locked component packages and source checksums are listed in desktop/Cargo.lock. Its MPL licence is in licenses/Symphonia-MPL-2.0.txt.

symphonia-adapter-libopus 0.3.0 is used under its MIT licence (licenses/Symphonia-Opus-MIT.txt); source: https://crates.io/crates/symphonia-adapter-libopus/0.3.0.

opusic-sys 0.7.6 and its bundled libopus are statically linked. Their notices are in licenses/Opusic-sys.txt and licenses/Opus.txt. Unchanged sources: https://crates.io/crates/opusic-sys/0.7.6.

Extended AAC decoding

fdk-aac-sys 0.5.0 statically links the unchanged Fraunhofer FDK AAC sources bundled by that crate. Its full notice is in licenses/FDK-AAC.txt. Corresponding pinned source package: https://crates.io/crates/fdk-aac-sys/0.5.0. Native integration uses the decoder only, on the audio control worker; callback mixing does not decode media. The original Rust adapter is in native/decoder/src/aac.rs.
