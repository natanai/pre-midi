# pre-midi 0.14.1-preview.38 Windows portable

## Changes since preview.37

- Apply scale/key pitch correction to solo vocals or pitched samples. Correction amount and response are in the Sound Inspector. Stretch, transpose, formants and reverse remain editable.
- Export Beats or Songs at 44.1, 48 or 96 kHz as 16-bit PCM, 24-bit PCM or 32-bit float WAV. A completed export shows peak, RMS, true-peak estimate and any sample overload. Integer WAV also offers dither.
- Movement routes can target stereo width and mid/side balance, set phase, follow the timeline or restart on each note or Clip, and use 2–16 step patterns.
- Filter, distortion and EQ can automate wet/dry mix and stepped enable. Play a Beat and use the lane's write gesture dial to record movement into its curve. EQ also shows the measured spectrum of the Sound preview.

## Install and test

Extract the whole ZIP to a writable folder and open pre-midi.exe. For a manual update, close the app and replace the application files. Keep your data/, projects/ and exports/ folders.

Try playing a Song, pausing, editing a Sound, seeking and resuming. Press Stop while a Song is preparing. Try pitch correction on one vocal phrase and export the same Song as 24-bit PCM and 32-bit float. This is a manual Windows 11 x64 portable preview. Physical audio and microphone testing remain open.
